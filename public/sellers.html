What agents check before paying you

Agents using x402 Doctor's pre-payment check (GET /api/v1/preflight, also in x402-safe-fetch) get go, caution or no_go. You get caution or no_go when:

  • a payment would fail: wrong amount units, an unknown token, a missing Solana token account, a bad EIP-712 domain;
  • the price is higher than your OpenAPI says, or the page isn't HTTPS;
  • you were payable on fewer than half of your scanned days;
  • several buyers reported failed payments after paying you.

Get found in the Bazaar

  1. Settle through the CDP facilitator (set your CDP API keys): resources that settle there (and declare discovery metadata) can be listed in the CDP Bazaar.
  2. Describe your resource: a clear description, input and output schema, serviceName, tags and an icon in your route config, and a /.well-known/x402.
  3. Stay payable: check yourself with step 1 after every deploy (or in CI: npx github:Fizzl13/x402-doctor <url> exits 1 on failures).
  4. Show it: the badge from step 2 on your README and site.
  5. Watch it: let the endpoint monitor (step 4) tell you on Telegram when it breaks, before your buyers notice.
