Pin your Agent Card

A Concordium badge is valid when the card at your agent's agent_uri hashes (SHA-256) to the metadata_hash on chain. This page hashes your card as it is served and sets both on chain with setAgentURI, signed in your own Concordium wallet. x402 Doctor never sees a key.

1. Your badge and card

2. Sign with the owner's Concordium wallet

Uses the Concordium Wallet for Web (Chrome or Brave), on the network of the badge, with the account that owns the agent.

3. Bind your EVM payTo to the agent (optional)

A payTo on Base or another EVM chain counts as the agent's when its key is bound with CIS-8 and the agent names that key (setExternalReference). First link the key to your Concordium account in Concordium's Agent Registry: Register Agent → Cross-Chain → your chain → Connect Wallet, Prove Ownership, Link Key, and stop there (no Sign & Register). Then come back here: MetaMask signs one message to show your public key (no transaction), and your Concordium wallet signs setExternalReference.
