What the scan checks

  • The endpoint answers 402 with a valid x402 challenge.
  • At least one payment option would settle: valid payTo and amount, the fee payer or EIP-712 domain, and for Solana a USDC token account at the payout wallet.
  • The price matches what its OpenAPI advertises, it is served over HTTPS, and it charges in a known USDC contract.

Agents get this track record in every pre-payment check (GET /api/v1/preflight, $0.001): a seller that was payable on fewer than half of its scanned days gets a caution.

For sellers

  • The scan sends one request (GET, or POST when you declared POST) per resource per day, plus one /openapi.json per origin, with at most two requests to your host at a time. It never pays and never sends a payment header.
  • User-Agent: x402-doctor-trust-scan/1.0 (+https://x402-doctor.onrender.com/trust; …)
  • Found a problem? Diagnose your endpoint for free on the web page, and see every check with a fix hint.
  • Want your origin left out of the scan? Open an issue.
Data: GET /api/trust?url=… (free, rate-limited) and GET /api/trust/summary. The full daily index is public on the trust-data branch.
