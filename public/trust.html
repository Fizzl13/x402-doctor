    What the scan checks

    • The endpoint answers 402 with a valid x402 challenge.
    • At least one payment option would settle: valid payTo and amount, the fee payer or EIP-712 domain, and for Solana a USDC token account at the payout wallet.
    • The price matches what its OpenAPI advertises, it is served over HTTPS, and it charges in a known USDC contract.
    • Its payout address (the x402 payTo or the MPP recipient) is not on the OFAC SDN sanctions list. Screened since 13 Oct 2026 against presign-guard's open copy of the US Treasury list; a listed address is no-go.

    Agents get this track record in every pre-payment check (GET /api/v1/preflight, $0.001): a seller that was payable on fewer than half of its scanned days gets a caution.

    • User-Agent: x402-doctor-trust-scan/1.0 (+https://x402-doctor.fizzl.eu/trust; …)
    • Get a badge for your README or site: look up your endpoint above and copy the snippet. It shows your track record (e.g. ) and links back here. For a whole domain use /badge.svg?url=https://your.domain.
