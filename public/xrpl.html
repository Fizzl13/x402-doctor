    What is checked on the ledger

    • The payout account exists. An XRPL account must be activated with the XRP reserve; a payment to an address that was never activated fails.
    • It accepts payments. No Deposit Authorization (which refuses payments from anyone not pre-authorised), and a destination tag in the challenge when the account requires one.
    • The trust line. For RLUSD (or another issued token) the payout account needs an unfrozen trust line to the issuer, or it cannot receive the token.
    • The real RLUSD. A token called RLUSD from another issuer than Ripple's (rMxCKbEDwqr76QuheSUMdEGf4B9xJ8m5De) is flagged.
    • Plus the x402 basics: a valid challenge, a valid r-address with checksum, XRP priced in drops and tokens in decimals.
