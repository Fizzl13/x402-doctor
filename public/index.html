x402 Doctor

Paste an x402-payable endpoint URL. Read-only diagnostics: no wallet, no payment, no risk.

Decoded challenge


  

    Checks the 402 challenge and its envelope, protocol version, every accepts[] option (network, asset, amount,
    payee, fee payer, EIP-712 domain), the resource URL and metadata, the Solana payout token account and facilitator,
    Bazaar discovery (including replaying the declared example), /openapi.json and the browser paywall.
    


    In CI or a terminal: npx github:Fizzl13/x402-doctor <url> (exits 1 on failures).
    


    x402 Trust Index: a daily check of every x402 resource in the CDP Bazaar and its 30-day track record.
    


    For agents: GET /api/v1/diagnose?url=<endpoint>, $0.01 USDC per call via x402 on Base or Solana, no rate limit
    (OpenAPI).
    


    Built while shipping PlainText and
    Ichimoku Signal — every check traces back to a bug that broke one of them.
