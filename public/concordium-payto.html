Bind your Algorand or Stellar payTo

x402 Doctor counts a payTo as your agent's when its key is bound with CIS-8 (Concordium's External Key Registry) to the account that owns your agent. On Algorand and Stellar the address is the key, so once it is bound there is nothing more to set: the payee check finds it. x402 Doctor never sees a secret key.

1. Your Concordium account

The account that owns your agent in Concordium's Agent Registry. Connect the Concordium Wallet for Web (Chrome or Brave), on the network of your agent.

2. Your payTo and the bytes to sign

Concordium lists Algorand and Stellar as tier 2: the CIS-8 contract checks a plain ed25519 signature over these bytes. Pera, Defly, Freighter and other wallets only sign a message with a prefix or hash around it (so a signature can never be a transaction), which the contract does not accept yet. So you sign on your own computer with cis8-sign.mjs: a short script with no dependencies that never goes online. Read it first. It asks for your Stellar secret key or Algorand mnemonic without showing it, stops unless the key is this payTo's, and signs only CIS-8 bytes. A key on a hardware wallet (Ledger) can't be bound this way.


      
    

  


  

    
3. Paste the signature and send the binding

    
The signature is checked here first. Then your Concordium wallet signs registerExternalKey on the CIS-8 contract (a normal transaction fee in CCD).
