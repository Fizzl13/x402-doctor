    How to read the city

    Every tower is one seller of paid APIs for AI agents: the site behind its x402 endpoints. A tower is as tall as its number of paid endpoints (on a log scale, so the largest don't hide the rest), and its floors are coloured by today's verdicts from the x402 Trust Index: green where a payment would settle as described, amber where it would but something is off, red where a careful agent should not pay, grey where the endpoint didn't answer. A red light on a roof marks a seller with at least one endpoint not to pay. The biggest sellers stand in the middle. The gate at the edge is the Fizzl Agent Wallet, where an agent comes in to buy: its arcs of light run only to sellers that are all green today.

    The city is built in your browser from the same data as the Trust Index and atlas.fizzl.eu, and it changes every day after the scan. Click a tower to see its counts and endpoints; each endpoint opens x402 Doctor's free check, never the endpoint itself.

    Before your agent pays

    The scan reads each 402 once a day. Your agent can ask before every payment: x402 Doctor checks one endpoint the way a paying agent would (free in the browser, $0.001 as a preflight for agents), and presign-guard checks the transaction or signature before it is signed.
