Set your agent wallet

ERC-8004 keeps one agent wallet per agent: the address it is paid at. Agents that verify payees compare it with the payTo in your 402. This page makes your payTo that wallet, in the Identity Registry on Base. Your wallet signs and sends everything; nothing goes through Fizzl.

1. Your agents and payTo

AgentOwnerAgent wallet now

2. Sign with the payTo wallet

Switch your wallet to the payTo account, then sign one message per agent. It proves you control that address. The signatures are valid for 4 minutes.

3. Send as the owner

Switch to the account that owns the agents and send one transaction per agent (a small Base gas fee).

Then run the web check on one of your paid routes: payee-identity should pass. Registry: 0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432 (ERC-8004 Identity Registry, Base).
