# x402 health, week ending 2026-10-05

Every resource in the CDP Bazaar and the MPP directory, checked daily the way a paying agent would (the 402 challenge only, never paid). Window: 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-05 (7 days).

## The numbers

- **35,306** resources on **2,129** hosts were checked on 2026-10-05.
- **93.9%** were clean: an agent could pay them without a second thought.
- **1.1%** (387) answered with a caution: testnet only, an unknown token, a URL mismatch and the like.
- **5%** (1,758) could not be paid at all. Payable, cautions included: 95% (2026-09-29: 95.8%).
- This week: **412** stopped being payable, **228** were fixed, **358** went back and forth, **6,098** were new.
- Median price of a payable call: **$0.01**.
- By protocol: x402 34,312 (96% clean, 97.1% payable), MPP 994 (22% clean, 23.4% payable). MPP endpoints come from the MPP directory (Stripe + Tempo).
- Left out: **84** test, staging or tunnel endpoints on 52 hosts (addresses such as staging., sandbox., testnet, ngrok or Vercel previews).

## Why payments would fail or need care

| Reason | Resources | What to fix |
|---|---:|---|
| Don't answer 402 at all, so there is nothing to pay | 1,748 | make the listed route answer 402 (a moved or renamed endpoint stays listed under its old URL until a payment goes through the new one) |
| Name another URL in their 402 than the one requested | 244 | build resource.url from your public origin (behind a TLS proxy: trust proxy, so it stays https) |
| Offer only testnet payment options | 125 | add a mainnet option (for example Base, eip155:8453) or delist the test endpoint |
| Send an MPP challenge without an expiry | 10 | set expires on each challenge (a few minutes ahead), so a captured challenge cannot be replayed later |
| Charge more than their OpenAPI advertises | 9 | keep the price in openapi.json in step with the 402 |
| Write the amount as dollars ("0.01") instead of atomic units | 6 | use atomic units: "10000" is $0.01 USDC |
