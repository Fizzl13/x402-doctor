# x402 health, week ending 2026-11-09

Every resource in the CDP Bazaar and the MPP directory, checked daily the way a paying agent would (the 402 challenge only, never paid). Window: 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-09 (7 days).

## The numbers

- **40,608** resources on **4,313** hosts were checked on 2026-11-09.
- **87.8%** were clean: an agent could pay them without a second thought.
- **7.7%** (3,115) answered with a caution: testnet only, an unknown token, a URL mismatch and the like.
- **4.5%** (1,842) could not be paid at all. Payable, cautions included: 95.5% (2026-11-03: 95.7%).
- This week: **507** stopped being payable, **282** were fixed, **138** went back and forth, **600** were new.
- Median price of a payable call: **$0.01**.
- By protocol: x402 40,179 (88% clean, 95.5% payable), MPP 429 (64.6% clean, 87.9% payable). MPP endpoints come from the MPP directory (Stripe + Tempo).
- Not counted: **2,047** endpoints without example input to judge them by: MPP endpoints that check their input before asking for payment (for example "symbol is required"), and listed URL templates (/wallet/:address) whose listing gives no example value.
- Left out: **232** test, staging or tunnel endpoints on 122 hosts (addresses such as staging., sandbox., testnet, ngrok or Vercel previews).
