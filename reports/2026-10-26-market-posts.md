Where AI agents' money goes in x402, week of 2026-10-26.

Coinbase's CDP Bazaar counted 897,959 paid calls over the last 30 days. 577,835 of them (64.3%) are on routes that were still paid in the last 3 days; the rest came from routes that stopped, often campaigns or quests.

The biggest single route this month: AI models & text generation: 206,012 paid calls from 2,083 wallets, 99 per wallet (22.9% of the market), last paid 2026-10-21. A few wallets paying many times can make a whole kind of service look big, so count wallets, not just calls.

Still paid, by kind: Crypto prices & trading 34.7%, Other data & lookups 31%, AI models & text generation 13.3%, Search, web & news 10.6%.

Rising this week, in still-paid calls: datapackvibe.com (+61,979), onesource.io (+7,182) and api-xpay.com (+4,714).
