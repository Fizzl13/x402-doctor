## How to read this

The Bazaar counts settled calls per route over a rolling 30 days, so a weekly change is the week that came in minus the week that dropped out. Unique payers are per route; a wallet that paid two routes counts twice. Every seller as a live map: https://atlas.fizzl.eu. Each kind's largest-seller share: https://lab.fizzl.eu.
