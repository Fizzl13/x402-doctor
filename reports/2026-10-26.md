# x402 health, week ending 2026-10-26

Every resource in the CDP Bazaar and the MPP directory, checked daily the way a paying agent would (the 402 challenge only, never paid). Window: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-26 (7 days).

## The numbers

- **70,654** resources on **3,622** hosts were checked on 2026-10-26.
- **94.4%** were clean: an agent could pay them without a second thought.
- **3.8%** (2,652) answered with a caution: testnet only, an unknown token, a URL mismatch and the like.
- **1.9%** (1,328) could not be paid at all. Payable, cautions included: 98.1% (2026-10-20: 95.3%).
- This week: **216** stopped being payable, **522** were fixed, **267** went back and forth, **20,212** were new.
- Median price of a payable call: **$0.01**.
- By protocol: x402 70,243 (94.5% clean, 98.2% payable), MPP 411 (64.7% clean, 91.5% payable). MPP endpoints come from the MPP directory (Stripe + Tempo).
- Not counted: **1,576** endpoints without example input to judge them by: MPP endpoints that check their input before asking for payment (for example "symbol is required"), and listed URL templates (/wallet/:address) whose listing gives no example value.
- Left out: **138** test, staging or tunnel endpoints on 96 hosts (addresses such as staging., sandbox., testnet, ngrok or Vercel previews).
- One host with 36,659 endpoints (51.9% of those checked) was 100% payable on 2026-10-26, so it moves the totals by itself. Without it: **88.3%** clean, 7.8% with a caution, 3.9% not payable, of 33,995 resources; 212 stopped being payable this week.

## Why payments would fail or need care

| Reason | Resources | What to fix |
|---|---:|---|
| Offer only Circle Gateway options, paid from a Gateway balance that standard x402 clients don't have | 1,693 | add a plain "exact" option next to Gateway if standard x402 agents should pay too |
| Don't answer 402 at all, so there is nothing to pay | 809 | make the listed route answer 402 (a moved or renamed endpoint stays listed under its old URL until a payment goes through the new one) |
| Name another URL in their 402 than the one requested | 448 | build resource.url from your public origin (behind a TLS proxy: trust proxy, so it stays https) |
| Timed out or could not be reached | 281 | check that the host is up and answers within 10 seconds |
| Ask to be paid in a token that isn't a known USDC contract | 195 | use the network's USDC contract as the asset |
| Offer only testnet payment options | 128 | add a mainnet option (for example Base, eip155:8453) or delist the test endpoint |
