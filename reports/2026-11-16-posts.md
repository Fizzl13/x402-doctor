## Reddit (r/x402)

**Title:** x402 health, week ending 2026-11-16: 88.8% of 43,000 Bazaar endpoints clean, 4.7% not payable

Every resource in the CDP Bazaar and the MPP directory, checked daily the way a paying agent would (the 402 challenge only, never paid). Window: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-16 (7 days).

## The numbers

- **43,000** resources on **4,416** hosts were checked on 2026-11-16.
- **88.8%** were clean: an agent could pay them without a second thought.
- **6.5%** (2,805) answered with a caution: testnet only, an unknown token, a URL mismatch and the like.
- **4.7%** (2,018) could not be paid at all. Payable, cautions included: 95.3% (2026-11-10: 95.6%).
- This week: **554** stopped being payable, **176** were fixed, **212** went back and forth, **526** were new.
- Median price of a payable call: **$0.01**.
- By protocol: x402 42,569 (89% clean, 95.4% payable), MPP 431 (63.6% clean, 86.8% payable). MPP endpoints come from the MPP directory (Stripe + Tempo).
- Not counted: **2,128** endpoints without example input to judge them by: MPP endpoints that check their input before asking for payment (for example "symbol is required"), and listed URL templates (/wallet/:address) whose listing gives no example value.
- Left out: **235** test, staging or tunnel endpoints on 115 hosts (addresses such as staging., sandbox., testnet, ngrok or Vercel previews).
